# Modified Charpy Sample

**Dimensions:** 10 × 10 × 55 mm base, plus 6 mm head feature (total ~61 mm length)

**Status: Head geometry updated (2026-08-17)** — Head feature changed from a round **button head** to a **T-slot** (square-shouldered tab) profile.

**Rationale:** The button-head geometry was hard to mass-produce; switching to a T-slot lets the samples be cut on the water jet in quantity (the water jet handles straight/square profiles far more easily and cheaply than the round button shoulder).

**Downstream impact:** This head-geometry change also required updating the [[Design/Mechanisms/Ceramic Mount|Ceramic Mount]] — the retaining slot now needs to extend a bit farther into the boron nitride cylinder to accommodate a square feature seated in what is otherwise a circular/cylindrical hole.
