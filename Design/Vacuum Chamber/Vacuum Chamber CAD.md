# Vacuum Chamber CAD Model

OnShape parametric model for the repurposed vacuum chamber and custom lid design.

## Quick Access

🔗 **OnShape Document**: https://cad.onshape.com/documents/c9bb59bfc991b1182ce6c971/w/cfd3d1ecd4a4ee8129c4b7d7/e/d7cfaa06d868d4390de19fd5?renderMode=0&uiState=6a740ff3605825dbb4df2ca2

## Model Contents

### Base Chamber Assembly
- Stainless steel bucket (existing)
- Acrylic lid (to be replaced/redesigned)
- Feedthrough ports and flanges
- Internal sample holder fixture

### Custom Lid Design  
- Press-fit seal centering
- Aluminum plate reinforcement pattern
- Coil lead pass-throughs
- Air control manifold interface
- Pressure gauge port

### Support Structure
- Scissor lift base mounting points
- Aluminum extrusion frame connections
- Load distribution analysis

## Usage

1. **View Assembly** — See how all components fit together
2. **Modify Dimensions** — Change bucket constraints or lid geometry
3. **Generate Drawings** — Export 2D manufacturing drawings
4. **Test Fits** — Verify coil clearance and sample positioning
